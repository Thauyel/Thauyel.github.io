How to play

Move. Survive. Grow. You only steer: every weapon fires on its own. The swarm grows every minute. Hold out until 15:00 to clear the stage.

  • Gems dropped by enemies give experience. Each level-up offers new weapons, upgrades and support modules. You can hold six weapons and six modules.
  • Evolutions: raise a weapon to max level, own its paired module, then open a cache dropped by an elite or boss. Paired modules are shown on every card and in the Codex.
  • Relics are powerful run-wide modifiers. Pick one at launch and more from the bosses at 6:00 and 12:00.
  • Reroll, Skip and Banish have limited charges. Banish removes an item from the pool for the rest of the run.
  • Break supply crates for repairs, credits, vacuums, EMPs and stasis fields.
  • At 15:00 every enemy is purged and The Purger arrives. It cannot be destroyed. Overtime is worth extra score.
